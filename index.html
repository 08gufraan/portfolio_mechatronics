About Me

Engineer by training,
problem-solver by instinct.

I'm Gufran Momin — a Mechatronics and Automation Engineer with 2.5+ years of hands-on experience in manufacturing process engineering. I work at Uno Minda Limited, 2-Wheeler Lighting Division, Pune, driving efficiency, quality, and productivity on the shop floor.

My expertise spans PLC ladder logic programming, HMI development (Mitsubishi), robot programming (Yaskawa), and process improvement through 5S, Kaizen, and Fixture Modification. I've implemented sensor-based Poka Yoke systems that meaningfully reduced defect rates across the assembly line.

I hold a B.Tech. in Mechatronics and Automation and am continuously expanding — currently exploring machine vision systems (Cognex & Keyence), Python, Industry 4.0 and Digital Twins of Assembly Lines. Outside the plant, I enjoy programming microcontrollers as a hobby.
